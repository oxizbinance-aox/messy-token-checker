    How the score works

    Protocol · 60 pts

    Contract safety from GoPlus: honeypot (auto-RUG), verified source, mintable, hidden owner, self-destruct, proxy, blacklist, taxes, deployer history — plus pair age and website/socials from Dexscreener.

    Holder · 25 pts

    Top-10 holder concentration from GoPlus, plus holder count and LP holders. ≤20% concentrated scores full marks; >80% scores zero.

    Liquidity · 15 pts

    Total liquidity across all Dexscreener pairs, 24h volume, and buy/sell balance. ≥$100k liquidity scores full marks; a dead pair scores zero.

    Verdict bands: SAFE ≥ 70 · WATCH 45–69 · RUG < 45

    Data: Dexscreener (market + liquidity) and GoPlus Security (contract + holders) — both free, keyless, live. The score is computed locally by a deterministic rule engine; the AI only explains it in plain language.

    Honest limitations — read before you trust a score
    • USDC, WETH and other quote tokens can't be checked. Dexscreener's token-pairs endpoint only sees tokens that are the base token of a pair — quote tokens return no pairs at all. That's a data blind spot, not a bug.
    • Top-10 concentration is a proxy. The full holder distribution is a paid endpoint; we score what free data can see.
    • LP lock status can't be verified from free data. We never invent a lock date. If the top LP holder is the burn address we say so; otherwise we say it's unverifiable.
    • Ownership-renounced status can't be verified from free data. Owner-risk flags are the closest proxy.
    • If GoPlus is down, a SAFE is capped at WATCH. Without contract + holder data we won't call a token safe — the score above stays computed, the verdict is capped until the contract can be verified.
    • On-device AI needs Chrome on Desktop v139+ with the Prompt API flag enabled, and a one-time model download. Everywhere else the cloud fallback runs instead.
